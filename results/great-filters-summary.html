Two filters in series
First filter — historical

Cumulative text a community ever produced: population × literacy × per-capita output × time. Latin and Sanskrit cleared it millennia ago; Old English never did. Dominated by the print and digital eras (1800–2000), not the manuscript age.

Second filter — infrastructural

Share of those tokens that exist today as clean, machine-readable Unicode a crawler can ingest. Governed by printing, encoding, OCR, online culture, and which states fund digitization. Modern Hebrew passed; Yoruba and Hausa, with similar speaker counts, did not.
